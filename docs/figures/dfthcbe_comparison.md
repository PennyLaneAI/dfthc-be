## Most important differences

1. Junk X-measured right after each prepare (columns J), on the `angles` wires (Q-1 variant (b),
   `walk.py:21-29`); dfthcbe keeps the junk to the erase. Layout/measurement timing; 0 T.
2. Unprepares from outcomes (step 21): only the 26 target qubits are measured there; the fix-up
   uses both outcome vectors and borrows 102 reset `angles` wires as one-hot (`onehot-pad` +16).
3. The inner entry carries `r` through the alias swap network (`flat-r`, +60 per inner read).
4. Comparator adjoints and adjoint uniform preparations are measured uncomputes, enabled by
   persistent dirty `cmp_work_*`/`succ_work_*` registers (`cmp-free`, −22 per inner unprepare).
5. Angle erase (step 18) is four ordered sub-steps with an SF fix-up enabled on G0=0
   (`sf-enable` +1, `onehot-pad` +4); exact unary iteration throughout (`exact-iter`, `no-prune`).
6. Spin multiplexer is 1 Toffoli instead of 2 (`mux` −1 per application).
7. Angle registers are b_rot−1 bits wide (742 vs 795 wires); no count change vs dfthcbe.
8. Not established from code: dfthcbe's exact comparator direction (image "<" vs our "<="), and
   whether the image's b_1 equals our d1 = 24 (b_2 = w_b + b_k2 = 20 per `resources.md:412`,
   so A_b = b_2 + 2 = 22 matches ours).
